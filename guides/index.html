Scanning, restoring and saving your family’s old photos

  • How to scan a photo album with your iPhone

    Lay the album flat near a window, hold your iPhone level above the page, and photograph each page without flash.

  • How to fix an old photo that turned yellow or orange

    The color shift comes from the print’s dyes fading at different speeds, and it can be corrected on a scan: the color is rebalanced until whites are white and skin looks like skin again.

  • How to restore a torn or creased photo

    Don’t tape it. Scan the print with the torn pieces laid together, and repair the scan: a crease or a clean tear can be made nearly invisible on the copy, while the print itself stays safe in an envelope.

  • What AI photo restoration can and can’t do

    AI restoration is good at damage: fading, color casts, scratches, dust and creases.

  • What to do with your parents’ old photo albums

    Scan them before you sort, split or store them, and ask who is in the pictures while someone still knows.

  • How to save the names and stories behind old photos

    Ask now, while someone still knows, and keep the answer with the photo itself.
