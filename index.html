Questions

Is it really free?

Your first 15 photos are free: restored, and yours to keep. You can start without signing in. Free photos you save or share carry a small “Made with Our Decades” mark; the yearly plan or the 100 photos removes it

Do I need a scanner?

No. Your iPhone’s camera is the scanner. Point it at a whole album page and every print on it becomes its own photo. Old pictures already on your iPhone, or in a WhatsApp chat, work too

Will it change how people look?

Every restored face is checked against the original, and we’d rather do less than turn someone into a stranger. The original scan is always kept: hold a photo to see it

How do I cancel, or delete everything?

Cancel the yearly plan any time in your iPhone’s Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions. What you made stays yours. To remove your account and every photo, story and video: Settings in the app, “Delete my account and photos”. Deleted means deleted

When can I get it?

Our Decades is coming to the App Store this week, on iPhone, in the United States. Write to hello@our-decades.com and we’ll tell you the day it’s live
