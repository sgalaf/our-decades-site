Privacy

Your family's photos are yours. Here is everything we do with them.

What we keep

  • The photos you scan or add, and the restored copies we make.
  • The stories you record or type, and the names and dates you add.
  • Your name and email from Apple or Google, if you sign in.

Who sees it

  • Only the people in your family on Our Decades, and the people you send things to.
  • Restoring happens on our own servers.
  • When a photo is brought to life, that photo is sent to our animation service (Kling, through fal.ai) to make the video. We ask them to delete it within an hour.
  • Your voice is turned into words on your iPhone. The words of a story (never the photo or the voice) are read by Claude, from Anthropic, to suggest names, dates and places for that photo. Anthropic doesn't use them to train its AI.
  • Payments go through Apple. We never see your card.

What we never do

  • Use your photos to train AI.
  • Sell or share your data for ads.

Deleting

Settings → Delete my account and photos removes your photos, stories, videos and the families you made, from our servers, right away.
