What's free

The first page you scan is free. Sign in and your next 15 photos are free too. What you save or share from them carries a small "Made with Our Decades" mark; a plan or a pack takes it off.

The 100-photo pack

Bought once, it's not a subscription: nothing renews, and its 100 photos never expire. Refunds go through Apple, as for the plan (below).

Cancel your plan

The yearly plan renews through your Apple ID until you cancel it. In the app: Settings › Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions, or Apple's subscriptions page. Everything you made stays yours.

Refunds

Apple takes the payments, so refunds go through Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com

New iPhone, or your plan doesn't show

Sign in with the same Apple or Google account, then tap "I already have a plan" (in Settings, or under the plans).

Delete everything

In the app: Settings › Delete my account and photos. Your photos, stories and videos, and the families you made, are deleted from our servers right away, for good. Deleting doesn't cancel the plan: cancel it with Apple too.
